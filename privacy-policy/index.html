Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what information Toolbench (“the site”, “we”) handles when you use it, and what it does not. It is written to be read in full in two minutes.

The short version

The calculators run entirely in your browser. We do not ask for an account, we do not receive the numbers you type, and we do not keep a history of your calculations. There are no analytics scripts on this site and no advertising scripts at present.

Information you enter into the calculators

Values you type — dimensions, prices, fee rates, DPI and sensitivity — are processed locally by JavaScript in your browser. They are not transmitted to us, not stored on a server, and disappear when you close or reload the page. Some tools may keep the last value in the page’s memory while it is open; nothing is written to persistent storage.

Information stored on your device

The site saves one preference in your browser’s local storage: whether you chose the light or dark theme. This value stays on your device and is not sent anywhere. It contains no identifier and no personal information. Clearing your browser’s site data removes it.

Server logs

Toolbench is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub’s infrastructure may record standard technical information about requests, such as the IP address, the requested file, the referring page and the user agent, for security and operational purposes. We do not have access to a visitor-level analytics dashboard and do not use those logs to identify individuals. GitHub’s own privacy statement governs its processing: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Cookies

The site sets no cookies of its own and does not use tracking pixels, fingerprinting scripts or third-party analytics.

Advertising

At the time of this update the site displays no advertising. If display advertising is introduced in the future — for example through Google AdSense — third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. In that case:

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info and, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Where the law requires it, a consent prompt will appear before any non-essential cookie is set, and refusing consent will not limit your use of the calculators.

This section will be updated and the “last updated” date changed before any such advertising goes live.

External links

Pages on this site link to external websites, including GitHub and, in the advertising section above, Google’s ad settings. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

Children

The site is a set of general-purpose calculators and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal information through the calculators, there is no stored profile to access, correct or delete. If you have contacted us by email or through the GitHub repository, that correspondence exists in those systems and you may ask us to delete our copy by replying to it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA; requests can be sent through the contact page.

Changes to this policy

If the way the site handles information changes — for example if advertising, comments or an email newsletter are added — this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will be described on the page itself, not only in the date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
